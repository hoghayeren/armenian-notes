## **Lesson Objectives**
* Learn how to conjugate verbs in the past continuous (aka imperfect/past progressive) tense in Western Armenian.

## **What is Past Continuous Tense and When Do I Use It?**
**Past continuous tense** (also known as **past progressive** or **imperfect**) is used when you want to describe an *ongoing action that was happening in a specific moment in the past*. For example, "I was playing football yesterday", "We were watching TV", or "She was cooking dinner". Notice in English that we use the words "was/were" and our verbs end in "-ing" which indicates it was an ongoing action..

## **Conjugating Verbs in Past Continuous Tense**

Conjugating Western Armenian verbs in the past continuous tense is similar to the present tense in that the կը particle is placed before the conjugated verb. The particle follows the same rules as the present tense: if the verb starts with a vowel, կը → կ՚. For տալ, գալ, and լալ, կը → կու. The four infinitives are also all conjugated differently, but this time the endings are different than in the present. These conjugations are shown below.

### First Group Verbs: -ել
{: style="margin: 0 !important; line-height: normal !important; font-size: 1.3em !important; font-weight: bold !important;" }

</div>

Conjugating -ել verbs in the imperfect happens by writing the Կը particle, then dropping the -ել ending of the verb, and replacing it with **-էի**, **-էիր**, **-էր**, **-էինք**, **-էիք**, or **-էին** based on the person. Here is an example with the verb *սիրել (to love/like)*.

|            | Singular | Translation | Plural | Translation |
| ---------- | ---------| ------------| ------ | ----------- |
| 1st Person | Ես կը սիր**էի**| I was liking | Մենք կը սիր**էինք** | We were liking|
| ---------- | --------------| ---------| --------------| ---------|
| 2nd Person | Դուն կը սիր**էիր** | You (inf., sing.) were liking | Դուք կը սիր**էիք** | You (form., pl.) were liking |
| ---------- | --------------| ---------| --------------| ---------|
| 3rd Person | Ան or ինք կը սիր**էր** | He/she/it was liking | Անոնք or իրենց կը սիր**էին** | They were liking |

## **The Addition of կոր**
Just like in the present tense, **կոր** can follow any of the conjugated imperfect tenses in spoken Western Armenian; for example, *կը սիրէի կոր*, *կը կարդայիր կոր*, and *կը խօսէր կոր*. The addition of կոր to the imperfect tense indicates a more continuous and habitual action, just as it does with the present. The simplest way to translate it into English is with “I used to…”; for example, “I used to love,” "you used to read,” and “he used to speak.”
