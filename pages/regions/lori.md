## **About Lori**
**Capital:** Vanadzor (Վանաձոր) <br>
**Location:** Northern Armenia, bordering Georgia
<br>

Lori is one of Armenia's greenest and most picturesque provinces, known for its lush forests, deep river gorges, and medieval monasteries. Located along the Georgian border, it's a region of beautiful mountain scenery, charming villages, and rich cultural traditions. Lori also holds a special place in Armenian literature and is known for its distinctive local cuisine.
<br>

## **Places to Know**
* **Haghpat and Sanahin Monasteries:** Two UNESCO World Heritage monastery complexes dating back to the 10th century. Both were important centers of learning and culture in medieval Armenia.
* **Stepanavan Dendropark:** A peaceful botanical garden established in 1931, featuring a wide variety of trees and plants, including species introduced from other parts of the world.
* **Dsegh Village:** The birthplace of Hovahannes Tumanyan, one of Armenia's most beloved poets and writers. His childhood home is now a museum.
<br>

## **Did You Know?**
* **A region with its own flavors:**  Lori is known for traditional dishes made with locally gathered wild greens, including փիփերթով ապուր (mallow soup). The region even celebrates its edible plants through a local festival!
* **A literary birthplace:** Besides Hovhannes Tumanyan, Lori is also the birthplace of Hrant Matevosyan, another celebrated Armenian writer.
* **A city with three names:** Lori's capital, Vanadzor, was previously called Kirovakan and, before that, Gharakilisa, meaning "Black Church."

* Ձոր (Dzor) — Gorge
