## **Conjugating Verbs in the Future Tense**
The future tense in Eastern Armenian has caused me a great deal of confusion for a while, but I will try my best to explain it here. The challenge arose when I discovered that there were two different ways to express future tense. The first way that I learned is called the **indicative** mood of future tense which involves adding the future participle **-ու** at the end of the verb infinitive and then tacking on the present tense auxiliary verb. The other way which is called the **optative/conditional** mood was discovered during my first session with my tutor after I had expressed that I felt confident in my ability to conjugate in the future tense. To my dismay, I had never seen the future tense conjugation that she had shown me which involves adding the prefix **կ-** to the verb in present form (more on this later).
